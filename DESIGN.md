# Emote style

- Aim for the existing Clash Royale chicken emote’s expressive, polished cartoon feel. Emotes should act, not just move text around.
- Use bold, readable silhouettes and sculpted artwork: berry-pink faces, dark plum depth, narrow cream highlights, and thick contours. For words, use chunky connected cursive with distinct letters.
- Layer artwork over the white, dark-outlined speech bubble. Let an ascender such as the P overlap the top rim; keep the bubble tail visible. Check readability at actual emote size.
- Give each animation an entrance, reaction, and clean hold. Use staggered left-to-right fades, cascading motion, squash and stretch, overshoot, and quickly damped wobble. Keep lettering coherent throughout deformation.
- Coordinate a subtle bubble recoil and small impact puffs or accents with the landing. Effects support the reaction; avoid constant sparkle or idle motion.
- Keep active motion around 1.5 seconds. Longer holds in review previews are for comparison, not extra animation.
- Approved direction: Perso’s Jelly landing and Domino entrance. The implemented Perso is Jelly Reveal with half the drop height: left-to-right fade, short drop, squash, and damped wobble. Sag is optional character acting, not a default.
- Avoid plain-font wiggles, generic bounce presets, thin illegible script, and excessive distortion.
- Present alternatives together with replay, slow motion, scrubbing, and small-size previews. Get the user’s review before exporting or replacing game assets.

References: `public/emotes/chicken.webp` and `public/emotes/perso.webp`; local Perso studies in `output/perso-review/` (ignored by Git, may be absent).
